## &#128218; Glossary (plain English)

| Term | What it actually means |
|---|---|
| **QAV** | "Qualified Action Value" - a weighted count of valuable things visitors did (contact form = 1.0, listing conversion = 1.0, custom form = 0.6, registration = 0.35, call click = 0.25). It is NOT a lead count; it's proof the page converts. |
| **Opportunity Score (0-100)** | How attractive an ad for this page looks, combining demand, conversion proof, ranking gap, audience, and readiness. |
| **Evidence grade (A-D)** | How much real proof backs the score. A = strong proof; D = mostly a bet. Missing data lowers the grade - it never earns free points. |
| **Channel Readiness** | Whether we can actually measure an Adwerx campaign for this page (tracking, destination, attribution) - separate from how good the idea is. |
| **Position gap** | Where the page ranks on Google. Ranking #1-3 = ads add little. Ranking #5-10 = the sweet spot where ads capture searchers organic misses. |
| **Visibility-only momentum** | Clicks went up but the number of people searching (impressions) did not - we got better at catching demand, demand itself isn't growing. Capped at 5 points. |
| **WATCH** | Interesting but unproven - the engine tracks it weekly and will promote it the moment evidence appears. Zero spend. |
| **PILOT** | A never-advertised destination. The first cycle exists to verify delivery and measurement, not to prove marketing ROI. |
| **Held budget** | Money under the monthly cap that nothing earned. It stays in the bank - the engine never spends just because budget exists. |
