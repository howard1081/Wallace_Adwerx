## Read on the numbers

- **Against the local Knoxville/East-TN field, Wallace leads by a wide margin**: 7.7K organic keywords and ~41.5K estimated monthly organic visits vs 1K–5K keywords and ≤4.1K visits for every local competitor. Wallace's traffic is roughly **10× the nearest local rival** (tnsmokymtnrealty.com).
- **lakefrontliving.com is the one exception** — but it is a national lakefront-listings portal, not a local brokerage. It ranks for more keywords (25.7K) nationwide while producing comparable traffic; it competes on the lakefront niche only.
- **tnrealestate.auction** ranks for many keywords (6K) but converts them into almost no traffic (729/mo) — long-tail rankings with no real visibility.
- **Nobody in this set is buying Google search ads** except lakefrontlainey.com (7 paid keywords). Paid-search competition on Wallace's keywords is essentially zero.
- Wallace's keyword count and traffic are both **growing** (+2.7% / +4.7% MoM) while most local competitors are flat or declining.

## Caveats

- SEMrush traffic figures are model estimates, not analytics data; use GA4 for Wallace's actual traffic.
- This is a baseline snapshot, not a scored input — SEMrush competitor context never affects the opportunity score (see engine docs).
- Keyword counts are US-database totals; overlap with Wallace's own keywords is much smaller (see the competitor-watch section of the weekly brief for shared-keyword counts).
